## Portfolio design and staged admission

Three credible shapes: extend each existing family independently (smallest migration, repeated interpretation mistakes); build a universal planner now (shared machinery, excessive semantic/ownership risk); or use **consumer packets over a small shared typed boundary**, recommended. Share exact quantity roles, source identities, domains, uncertainty/status and execution budgets; keep family models/checkers owned locally. Each packet names one usable workflow, its interpretation and actual serving path. Add common abstractions only after two consumers expose the same need.

For each packet: (1) freeze exact baseline sources/artifact and independently authored truth cases; (2) explain every failure as interpretation, model, data, execution or scorer disagreement; (3) freeze candidate before opening fresh generator seeds; (4) compare shipped own-scorer results, two fresh seeds and independent cases separately; (5) exercise actual default host composition and relocation on affected platforms. Release-build latency, whole-process memory and concurrent tails answer performance questions; cold isolated kernel timing does not substitute. Negative results—no new reader answers, statistical assumptions unusable, view maintenance slower, honest applicability causing unanswered tasks—should reject or narrow designs rather than disappear from reporting.
