# Statistical specialists: make the question and uncertainty part of the input

Research/design scout, 2026-10-31. No implementation, builds, tests or commits. **No new specialist-panel numbers.** Recommendation: first expose the existing supplied calibration consumer faithfully; then build an events-and-exposure GLM specialist and a separate sequential bounded-measurement specialist. Prediction regularization belongs behind these, driven by an actual dataset, rather than becoming the default explanation of every statistics panel.

## What exists

`native/oracle-statistics/src/lib.rs::regress` fits supplied dense rows by thin faer SVD, with required intercept and covariance choices. `RegressionInput` carries response, fixed positive relative precisions, named predictors/units, caller assumptions and prediction rows. The operation is `statistics.linear_regression`; `call` uses a bounded JSON envelope. `faer=0.24.4` and `statrs=0.18.0` are pinned with defaults disabled. Admission caps are 512 observations, 64 parameters, 128 predictions, n*p*p <= 2,097,152 and 32 MiB SVD scratch; `Par::Seq` is explicit. Cancellation surrounds, but cannot interrupt, SVD/quantiles.

Weights are divided by their maximum and rows multiplied by square roots. Global design/response scaling limits range loss. SVD truncation uses relative rank tolerance; it is coordinate-dependent. Returned residuals are recomputed from original rows and returned coefficients. Full rank, positive residual degrees of freedom, non-roundoff residual scale, condition <=1e8 and a stationarity policy gate inference. A deficient fit retains a minimum-norm candidate but withholds every new-row prediction, even an estimable one. A useful incremental capability is therefore **estimable contrasts**, tested against the retained row space; this need not precede the new specialists.

Classical covariance and Student-t inference require independent Gaussian errors with variance sigma²/w and a prespecified correct linear conditional mean. HC3 uses the sandwich of the transformed WLS problem, including squared loss weights in the meat, and asymptotic normal tails; it is not protection against dependence, selection or optional stopping. Classical observation intervals require a supplied new-observation precision. HC3 cannot invent that noise variance. Influence diagnostics are not calibrated outlier probabilities. These are good distinctions to preserve across every expansion.

`tools/oracle_components/native_statistics.py::NativeStatisticsOperations` already declares/validates the structured operation and distinguishes identified conditional inference, unidentified fits and fits without inference. `native/oracle-python/src/providers.rs` exports feature-gated catalog/call functions. The README's claim of no PyO3 integration is therefore stale; feature availability and structured registration do not establish end-to-end specialist routing or current panel success.

The actual shipped `upstream/root/specialists/statistics{,-v2,-v3}` panels cover mean, median, range, sample/population dispersion, combinations, permutations, supplied z-scores and binomial point probabilities, plus declines. Stored heldout **specialist** results are respectively 126/142, 132/144 and 142/144, each with 2 false-yes answers. These September 29 verdicts are historical, not fresh native measurements; the lower `domain.head` numbers are a different system. None measures regression, GLMs or uncertainty. Preserve the descriptive/counting consumer and score any new specialist on its own tasks.

`src/extract/geostats/GeoStats_Paired.thy` separately folds exact rational paired moments. `line_fit_normal_equations` states that a returned line has zero residual sum and zero x-weighted residual sum; `line_fit_least_squares` states the SSE decomposition and global minimum. That is real algebraic content, not a sampling theorem. Its rational moment subtraction is exact; copying n*sxx-sx² into floating-point streaming code would introduce catastrophic cancellation. Weighted summaries, exact line fitting and geometry remain legitimate separate consumers.

## Ranked designs

### 1. Events and exposure: binomial-logit first, Poisson-log second

Concrete questions: “How does failure probability vary with supplied stress and batch indicator?” and “How does event rate vary with operating conditions after accounting for device-hours?” Response support and exposure are integral to these questions. OLS probabilities outside [0,1] and log(count+1) regressions are inadequate substitutes.

Represent binomial rows as integer `successes`, positive integer `trials`, supplied design; Poisson rows as nonnegative integer counts plus positive exposure mapped to a fixed log offset. Keep trial counts distinct from precision/loss weights. State the independent sampling unit and grouping explicitly; repeated measurements from one device cannot silently become independent rows. Own family/link/offset validation in the statistics specialist; source extraction owns which observations are present and whether the supplied table is complete.

Use bounded Fisher scoring/IRLS with QR or SVD subproblems, stable logistic/log-likelihood evaluation, step halving and explicit iteration/work caps. Reuse faer's existing numerical stack rather than adopting a dataset framework. Argmin 0.11 provides useful optimizer termination/line-search machinery, but does not provide response validation, separation detection or inferential authority; adopt it only if shared nonlinear consumers justify the interface. Report score norm, likelihood/deviance, rank and information conditioning as distinct diagnostics. Tiny step size alone is not successful estimation.

Unpenalized logistic MLE can be infinite under complete or quasi-separation. A finite iteration limit must yield `no_finite_mle_established` or `not_converged`, not a large coefficient with a reassuring Wald p-value. First ship ordinary MLE with explicit failure modes and ordinary asymptotic uncertainty on well-behaved cases. Then add **explicitly selected** Jeffreys/Firth penalization for sparse events. Kosmidis–Firth's finiteness result assumes full-rank design; it does not rescue aliased parameters. Their §2.2 also explains why finite penalized Wald intervals can fail badly for extreme true coefficients. Do not describe bias reduction as exact coverage, or silently switch estimands when MLE fails. Profile calculations are a later bounded operation, still approximate, not an automatic coverage guarantee.

Poisson information assumes the specified mean/variance model; extra-Poisson dispersion, excess zeros and correlated event streams require another model. Do not return Gaussian individual prediction intervals for counts. Initial output should be fitted probabilities/rates, conditional mean intervals and model checks, with no claim of causation.

### 2. Sequential bounded-measurement monitor

Consumer: accumulating independent quality-inspection outcomes, or bounded per-case benchmark success scores, where the user actually asks whether enough evidence has arrived. This is stronger than repeatedly calculating fixed-sample binomial intervals and treating the first favorable result as final. It is not a generic drift detector or arbitrary telemetry forecaster.

Start with a pure replayable operation over ordered observations in declared fixed bounds, alpha, fixed method/version and hypotheses. Return confidence-sequence endpoints and e-process/log-capital summaries. A host may later persist opaque bounded state; the numerical library should not own a global mutable experiment registry. Record sequence position, bounds, method, hypothesis and original alpha allocation. Retries must be idempotent at the host boundary. Do not merge two order-dependent betting states as if they were sufficient counts.

Waudby-Smith–Ramdas §2 assumes bounded observations with a common **conditional** mean given the past; IID sampling is sufficient but not necessary. Predictable betting rates must be chosen from past data. §3 gives simpler predictable plug-in supermartingales; use one explicit conservative construction first, then compare §4 betting variants if width is inadequate. Ville's inequality controls crossing at any time; fixed-time interval coverage does not. Evaluate capital in log space. A grid-only inversion may exclude the true mean between grid points: bracket roots and round interval endpoints outward, or report a conservative hull. Changing bounds after seeing outcomes, response-adaptive cherry-picking, or mean drift invalidates the claimed contract. Multiple metrics/arms need a declared joint error allocation or separate multiplicity method.

### 3. Regularized prediction, with streaming QR as a conditional extension

Consumer: many correlated calibration/sensor features, where stable heldout predictions matter more than individual coefficient significance. Start with explicit ridge and supplied lambda: solve augmented least squares [sqrt(W)X; sqrt(lambda)P] with an unpenalized intercept, retaining centering/scaling metadata for predictions. Penalty coordinates change the model; unlike present OLS, per-feature standardization changes regularization semantics and must be declared. Report prediction loss and effective complexity, without recycling unpenalized t tests.

Elastic net and a bounded lambda path become worthwhile when sparse feature selection is an actual workflow. Friedman–Hastie–Tibshirani motivates coordinate descent/warm starts; the current glmnet guide makes the penalty normalization and standardization choices concrete. Training-set feature selection followed by ordinary coefficient intervals is invalid. Supplied folds permit reproducible validation; repeated/grouped rows need group-aware splits and tuning evaluation needs an outer holdout. A successful optimizer is not evidence of generalization.

For genuinely long measurement streams, retain triangular QR state, transformed response and residual norm using block Householder/TSQR; take a small SVD of final R for rank policy. Demmel et al. §4.2 supplies the sequential reduction construction. This avoids storing all rows and avoids the conditioning squared by X'X. It cannot recover row-level influence or HC3 covariance from just R and residual sum: HC3 needs residual/leverage-dependent row information or a second pass. Consequently streaming is a separate reduced-output operation, not a transparent replacement of `regress`. Current 512-row caps make immediate TSQR engineering unjustified without a real larger consumer.

Huber fitting, bootstrap and Bayesian models rank below these. HC3 does not make coefficients resistant to contamination; Huber residual weighting does not solve high-leverage contamination. Bootstrap must choose pairs/residual/wild/cluster units consistent with the design, retain bounded RNG/replay semantics and report failed resamples; it cannot repair selection or dependence merely by resampling rows.

## Staged decisions and decisive rejection cases

1. **Consumer prerequisite:** make the existing calibration operation reachable with complete schema-to-native-to-answer preservation. Panels must distinguish fitted mean from new observation, null inference from refusal, loss weights from counts, and conditional association from causal effect. Rerun only affected shipped panels and add independent supplied-calibration truth cases. No score is claimed here.
2. **GLM experiment:** analytic intercept-only grouped binomial and Poisson-with-exposure identities; grouped versus expanded independent Bernoulli equivalence; covariate/offset transformations; deliberate complete/quasi-separation, duplicate columns, all-zero counts, huge offsets and exhausted iteration budget. Compare independent high-precision/reference calculations and likelihood gradients. Reject a backend that reports convergence from a small step with non-small score, or changes family/penalty silently.
3. **Sequential experiment:** exhaustively enumerate short Bernoulli paths and their probabilities; check the probability of *ever* excluding each generating mean rather than marginal coverage at a chosen n. Exercise endpoints, long identical runs, adaptive predictable rates, replay, and malicious current-observation-dependent bets. Reject numerics that overstate capital or shrink the inverted set inward. Simulation can refute implementation claims, not prove all-distribution coverage.
4. **Prediction/streaming experiment only after demand:** ridge lambda-to-zero on full-rank well-conditioned data; correlated/rank-deficient columns; parameter-unit changes with corresponding penalty transformation; leakage traps. For QR, large-offset nearly constant predictors must agree with batch SVD within declared numerical policy across partitions. Measure bounded memory, latency, cancellation and any second-pass cost on the designated host; no performance numbers invented.

Interfaces needed: routing owns operation choice and complete row binding; scientific models own Jacobians/physical units and distinguish deterministic bounds from covariance; statistics owns conditional fit/inference semantics. A nonlinear delta-method covariance is local/asymptotic, not automatically exact Student-t inference. Relations/source scouts should supply coverage and sampling provenance: completeness of observed records does not establish random sampling of a population.
