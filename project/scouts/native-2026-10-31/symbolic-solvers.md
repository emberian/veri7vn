# Symbolic solvers: checked design proposals with real consumers

Research only, 31 October 2026. No code, dependencies, builds, tests or commits changed. **No new specialist-panel numbers exist.** The recommendation is to add exact linear certificates before a general solver service, while extending the existing rational checker for a deliberately small formula-design specialist.

## What is already here

`native/oracle-reasoning/src/algebra.rs::optimize` runs egg 0.11 over integer `+,-,*`, extracts with multiply cost 4, and independently expands both expressions into sparse BigInt polynomials. Its cost is a declared tree proxy, not measured execution time or a global optimum. Expansion can dominate search or hit its explicit term/work caps.

The newer `formula.rs` is substantially stronger than the polynomial-only roadmap suggests: hash-consed `Dag`, exact `BigRational`, seven-SI dimensions, caller bounds, partial division semantics, interval facts and certificate replay. `formula_search.rs::Facts` carries dimensions, interval enclosures and totality through egg; explanations become `Certificate` steps with reflexivity, transitivity, congruence and named rules. `formula::replay` checks patterns and conditions independently. Translation/replay failure retains the original expression. `x/x→1` therefore needs a nonzero bound; `0*(1/x)` cannot casually become total zero. These are useful semantics to preserve, not an invitation to add unchecked identities.

`dimensions.rs` admits original input before optimization, preventing cancellation from concealing incompatible sums. Values must already be coherent linear SI; affine temperatures are external. `stoichiometry.rs::balance_reaction` performs exact rational RREF and returns the full nullspace of supplied atom/isotope/charge inventories. Nullity one permits an exact positivity decision; nullity greater than one searches bounded positive free coefficients (default bound 8, 4096 candidates). Exhaustion correctly means inconclusive. Conservation is not evidence that a reaction occurs, that species identities are real, or that missing reactants may be invented.

`graph.rs` separates petgraph forest search from an independent coverage/cycle-replacement checker. `shortest_path.rs::Certificate` separates finite paths with feasible potentials, outgoing-closed unreachable sets, and anchored negative cycles; irrelevant negative cycles do not invalidate a finite corridor. The graph is the complete *supplied* graph, not a complete world map.

`native/oracle-science/src/lib.rs::linear` uses faer SVD and recomputes original-row residuals. This is floating-point numerical acceptance, not exact infeasibility or algebraic-rank proof. Its unit strings are declarations. `thermal.rs::thermal_network` is already a real domain adapter: it owns positive-conductance topology, reservoir semantics, floating components, power balances and kelvin guards. Retain this domain ownership.

The extracted integration precedent is `src/extract/discrete-certificate/`: a resident process accepts Rust-selected input-edge occurrences, with separately operator-admitted proof/compiler receipts. Its HOL statement is conditional: `external_mst_cert vs es t` implies spanning, and for every spanning `t'`, `weight t ≤ weight t'`. Scope excludes disconnected graphs, singletons and omitted vertices; the Rust forest guarantee remains separate. This is not a generic graph-proof facility. `energy/Calorimetry.thy` already proves temperature intervals agree with endpoint differences and sensible unit energy balance. Reuse those conversions instead of recreating them numerically.

A dangerous interoperability detail: `src/extract/fusion/Fusion.thy` gives its sixth coordinate the meaning **money**, whereas native seven-SI coordinates put **amount** sixth. Fusion also has six coordinates, rather than native SI’s seven. Padding or truncating integer arrays cannot bridge these representations; require a named semantic conversion.

## Three credible designs

### 1. Exact linear feasibility and optimization certificates — first choice

Create a bounded rational problem representation owned by reasoning: stable variable/row IDs, exact coefficient strings, equality/inequality sense, units supplied through a checked adapter, objective, bounds and model provenance. Domain adapters own inventories, measurements and physical assumptions. Search can initially be a small existing external proposer, invoked through owned workers; choose a Rust library/FFI only after the consumer and certificate format are fixed. No crate installation is needed to establish the checker design.

Use standard form `min cᵀx`, `Ax=b`, `x≥0`. A witness `x` proves feasibility by exact substitution. A dual `y` with `Aᵀy≤c` gives a lower bound `bᵀy`; equality to `cᵀx` proves optimality. A Farkas witness `Aᵀy≥0`, `bᵀy<0` proves infeasibility. A feasible base plus recession direction `Ad=0`, `d≥0`, `cᵀd<0` proves unboundedness. General bounds and inequalities must be translated with a replayable variable/row map; checking only a transformed matrix risks proving the wrong question. Reconstruction of rationals from floats is a proposal and may fail; it never inherits numerical tolerance as exact truth.

The first consumer closes the stoichiometry gap: strict positive homogeneous balance exists iff `Az=0,z≥1` is feasible, by scaling a positive vector. With integer inventory coefficients, rational feasibility can produce positive integer coefficients by denominator clearing. GCD normalization is not minimum coefficient sum; minimum positive integer balance is a distinct integer optimization problem. Return an exact witness or checked infeasibility, otherwise unknown.

The same checker supports minimum-cost mixtures with composition bounds and stock limits, nonnegative concentration feasibility from response bounds, and capacitated resource flows. Concrete questions become “can these stocks make this blend?” and “what constraint makes this requested mixture impossible?” Checked dual bounds support price comparisons; infeasibility multipliers explain combinations of incompatible rows. Supplied coefficients and inventory completeness remain assumptions. Robust uncertain coefficients need a separately derived robust model, not substitution of midpoint values.

### 2. A typed formula-design specialist with bounded CEGIS

Reuse `Dag`, `Premises`, `Budget` and replay, keeping the checker independent of the search engine. Start with user-selected exact rational templates for force/energy/flow expressions; restrict the grammar to existing operators, declared variables and explicit nonzero bounds. The user supplies the target relation or an existing kernel-derived formula. Search may factor expressions, choose shared subexpressions or find an equivalent bounded template. Every admitted result needs the current replay proof or exact polynomial check, plus independently checked dimension/domain obligations.

CEGIS adds examples to reject bad templates cheaply, then asks a verifier for a universal counterexample. Examples alone prove nothing. For rational identities, numerator polynomial normalization plus explicit denominator-domain equivalence often avoids SMT entirely. SMT becomes useful for conditional piecewise formulas, integer component choices and constrained bounds; `unknown`, timeout or an uncheckable UNSAT result must retain unknown assurance. A model is replayed directly; a universal claim requires a supported proof certificate or complete finite enumeration. Do not expose a generic “SMT says verified” bit.

For numerical evaluation, rational equality does not preserve floating-point error, exceptions or overflow. A lower tree cost must not become a faster-or-more-accurate claim. Initially choose exact evaluation and report cost. Later measure repeated real consumer evaluation, including conversion/checking, and use a separate interval/error argument for floating code. Shared-DAG extraction and multiple objectives should follow evidence: the current tree cost double-counts reusable subexpressions.

### 3. Finite planning with independent schedule replay

A laboratory batch planner or maintenance/resource planner has integer start times, precedence, bounded machine availability and cumulative capacity constraints. CP/SAT is a better search representation than egraph rewriting. An accepted schedule is checked against original tasks: precedence, duration, resource overlap, inventory conservation and horizon. A feasible schedule is useful even without optimality. Claim a makespan optimum only with an independently checked lower bound matching it, exhaustive bounded search, or a supported proof trace. An LP relaxation lower bound alone usually leaves an integer gap.

Existing shortest-path potentials can certify difference constraints and lower bounds; they do not solve disjunctive machine allocation or cumulative capacity. MST is appropriate for a supplied undirected least-cost connection model, not automatically for directed logistics, capacity or reliability. Keep these specialists separate until their shared certificate machinery is demonstrated.

## Literature translated into decisions

Existing paperbin sources read selectively: Willsey et al., [egg (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03082), §§2 and 4, motivates analyses with well-defined joins, bounded saturation and explicit extraction costs. Current `Facts::merge` retains one enclosure on disjoint intersection and fills an absent dimension; it is untrusted search data, so this cannot admit a false result through replay. Before adding richer sign/convexity facts, define inconsistency and order-independent merges rather than using fallback values as mathematical facts. Alur et al., [Syntax-Guided Synthesis (2013)](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~alur/SyGuS13.pdf), synthesis/verification sections, supports a restricted grammar and a real verification oracle; it does not turn testing into universality.

New primary sources saved in `~/paperbin/oracle-native-20261031/symbolic-solvers/`, with URLs, hashes and retrieval dates in `manifest.json`:

- Boyd and Vandenberghe, [Convex Optimization (2004)](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf), selected §§5.2, 5.5, 5.8: distinguish primal witnesses, dual bounds, strong-duality assumptions and infeasibility certificates. Begin with rational LP. Unknown conductance times unknown temperature, variable resistance times current, nonlinear kinetics, radiation equality and integer choices are not automatically convex. Even convex-looking equality constraints require care.
- Perron, Didier and Gay, [The CP-SAT-LP Solver (2023)](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CP.2023.3), complete two-page invited-talk abstract and references: integer CP plus SAT, LP relaxation and portfolio workers fits scheduling; it is not an implementation tutorial or independently checked optimality interface. A native integration would require explicit worker caps and deployment review, not default uncontrolled portfolios.
- Ekici et al., [Extending SMTCoq (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05947), §§1–2: solver-specific witnesses can be translated by an untrusted preprocessor into small independently checked certificates. This architectural precedent fits the current egg replay and resident HOL boundary; its Coq checker is not an Isabelle drop-in.

## Sequence and decisive rejection cases

1. Specify canonical LP inputs and four certificate outcomes; validate on hand-derived tiny cases without selecting a solver. Mutate signs, row order, dimensions and denominators. Exhaustive small rational/integer instances must refute a buggy checker. Include infeasible zero rows, degenerate optima, both-primal-and-dual-infeasible cases and unbounded directions lacking feasible bases.
2. Wire only supplied-species positivity, preserving all old statuses and inventory/charge replay. Add its own panel generator and scorer, plus adversarial positivity witnesses beyond the old bound. Measure parsing, proposal, reconstruction, checking and complete reply cost. Reject a provider if exact reconstruction regularly fails on the intended small cases or checker growth dominates consumer benefit.
3. Connect one blend specialist, then optional bounded planner. Run shipped adjacent chemistry/quantity panels through the real adapter and record new specialist scores separately. No current score is claimed. Formula design proceeds independently with denominator-zero, affine-temperature and checker-budget counterexamples; do not require a general solver platform first.
4. Extract a small rational linear-certificate predicate to HOL after the Rust format stabilizes. Reuse the resident transport, operator receipt admission and cancellation infrastructure; do not duplicate process pools. State the theorem over original normalized rows and explicit domain premises. Reuse existing kernels to construct trusted unit conversions and evaluate established physical laws, while keeping untrusted prose recognition and model completeness visible.

Dependencies: scientific-domain scout owns thermal/transient physics and numerical acceptance; runtime scout owns contained proposer/checker workers; data scouts own complete inventories and source coverage. Open choices for root are the first concrete blend data source, whether integer-minimal reaction balances justify their additional complexity, and whether users need universal formula synthesis or simply checked simplification. These are product/engineering selections, not permission barriers.
