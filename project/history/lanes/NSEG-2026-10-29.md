# Native exact segment chains and one nested fragment pass

Owner: native_segment_reader. Consumer: `Image::read_whole` and Runtime's
shared language evaluator through `reader.rs` → `composition.rs` →
`segments.rs`.

## What is implemented

The native reader can build entity chains from exact learned segment rows and
put a pre-nested chain into one exact learned nested fragment. It keeps direct
whole-question and exact fragment readings, so a new interpretation cannot win
by hiding an older interpretation. Every successful alternative still reaches
the existing answer-form veto and final answer-agreement rule. Learned support
admits rows under the existing trust policy; it never elects an answer.

## Source behavior preserved

A segment spans at most 12 words. A chain contains 2–4 segments and at most one
source segment. The source goes first even when its words come after a filter.
Without a source, execution starts with `FindAll`. Concept-filter units precede
other filter units, preserving surface order within each group and preserving
qualifiers attached to each unit. Assembly gives each step its preceding step
as its dependency.

The ordinary exact fragment and nested fragment bounds remain 30 words. A chain
of four 12-word segments may span 48 words, so outer holes admit that source
family's larger span. This changes a language-family bound, not host budgets.
Nested fragment templates receive only base exact fragments or segment chains,
never another nested result. The native code does not invent recursive nesting.

Indexed prefixes prune impossible words/slots. Only a complete learned outer
question demands region work, and only a complete learned nested wording
demands its child regions. Unit lookups and base regions are memoized across
those questions. A mutable DFS path replaces copied frontier states. The host
combination cap counts admitted runnable/nonempty base region/program nodes
together with their child-region/program witnesses, then complete resolved
outer interpretations. Repeated equivalent unit paths share an
endpoint/role/program node. Unresolved outer holes, nested hole splits and
individual units remain bounded by fuel, bytes and row/match envelopes; they do
not spend the resolved interpretation cap. Genuinely different resolved
programs or child witnesses still spend that same cap. Host binding, program,
materialization and request-budget exhaustion return an error, never a
truncated answer set. The source's silently truncated frontiers are not copied.

Before executing anything, composition requires every hole in an outer program
to have complete syntax. It first validates the union of selected pre-nested
child programs, then validates admitted parent programs once per distinct
program with the caller's existing shared `LanguageWorkspace`. Child witnesses
are retained even when a parent skeleton omits its template's `@X` splice.
Nested syntax is retained as a delayed skeleton plus shared child-choice sets.
The complete outer syntax establishes demand; the shared semantic evaluator
checks the full consumed base union before any nested products are formed. An
empty child prevents that parent from being formed or running; an unsupported
child remains an error even when an empty child alternative produces the same
parent program. Deduplication includes child admission witnesses until those
checks complete. Unconsumed unsupported regions cannot poison an answer;
consumed unsupported operations remain explicit errors, including when an empty
sibling could otherwise erase them. Every entity stage, including a dead
branch, must be nonempty before a fragment is usable. A terminal outer `Count`
cannot turn an empty fragment into a zero answer.

## Scope and evidence

This remains an untrusted language recognizer. Missing source rows do not prove
absence or completeness. Exact matches are covered; content-key and strip-key
fallback, analogy, clause patches, date wrappers and deeper nesting remain
outside this coverage. The public coverage declaration includes
`exact_segment_and_single_nested_v1`; no full Reader7 claim is made.

The contracts cover source/filter reordering, all segment alternatives, exact
and segment children of nested templates, mixed whole/exact/segment/nested
answer disagreement, the one-pass boundary, source-free `FindAll`, two-source
rejection, qualifier attachment, stable concept-first order, four versus five
units, the 48-word chain bound, empty intermediate stages, unused and consumed
unsupported regions, form veto, resource exhaustion, cancellation, recovery and
worker lease cleanup. Five additional contracts cover omitted `@X` with empty,
unsupported and valid children, empty/unsupported alternatives yielding
identical parent programs, and repeated child-program admission cache reuse.
The independent KB has two York residents, one birth there and one shared
employer. Its nested employer count is 1, while its resident count is 2. One
malformed learned-row adversary deliberately attempts to resurrect an empty
child through an unrelated later branch.

Central execution of the earlier freeze passed all 23 segment contracts and 170
scoped Rust contracts, but the real paired 100 regressed from 36 correct / 0
errors to 33 correct / 3 errors (0 wrong, 4 refused and 60 unknown). Cases 200,
2688 and 5826 all failed with `resource limit: composition bindings`, retained
under `var/expanded-runtime-20261029/reader100/`. These are failed
qualification results, not a completion receipt. Static diagnosis found that
the new chart charged intermediate units, unresolved hole hits and duplicate
assembly paths as completed interpretations. The corrected source interns
equivalent typed units and charges the unchanged cap only for unique resolved
nodes, including independent child witnesses. Three new contracts cover 100
equivalent unit paths under cap 2, unresolved nested splits under cap 2 and
three genuinely distinct resolved programs exhausting cap 2. A fourth
accounting contract keeps identical child programs at distinct child region
boundaries as distinct capped witness nodes.

The accounting-only repair passed 27 focused contracts but still failed the
three real cases. A temporary central diagnostic, retained in
`var/expanded-runtime-20261029/segment-diagnostics.json`, established that all
failures occurred before nesting: case 200 had 400 syntactic base candidates in
one region; comparison regions had 248 variants each. These were not admitted
runnable/nonempty fragments. The current structural correction retains delayed
syntax recipes, establishes complete outer demand, independently checks the
full consumed base-program union through the shared semantic workspace, and
only then admits runnable/nonempty base nodes. It forms nested products only
from admitted children. Temporary diagnostic errors have been removed.

Segment provenance now retains each unit's exact wording, role and support,
while the typed program carries its executable steps once. Nested provenance
retains child regions/wording/support plus explicit child program witnesses; it
no longer serializes complete child fragments redundantly into its wording.
Three new dense contracts exercise 100 alternatives at each of two nested
holes: all empty children produce zero of the 10,000 possible parent products,
one viable choice per hole produces exactly one parent, and an unsupported
child remains visible beside the empty alternatives. Base viability checks are
shared across the two regions by program identity. The 30-contract structural
freeze passed centrally and restored cases 2688 and 5826. Case 200 then hit the
materialization bound. The temporary byte probe in
`var/expanded-runtime-20261029/segment-byte-diagnostics.json` measured 1.246 MB
of fragment/dedup accounting, 331 KB of raw parsed batches, 232 KB of
unit/dedup accounting and 265 KB of viability keys, with no admitted nodes. The
current walker streams each parsed result directly into deduplicated owned
storage. A transient result reservation transfers to its consumer rather than
remaining charged as a second live copy. Per-call dedup sets release their
reservation when dropped. Retained charts/programs/semantic cache remain
charged; request fuel stays cumulative. No host bound was raised. One
additional 1,000-alternative contract checks that equivalent streamed matches
fit a 200,000-byte live-storage envelope while retaining the distinct-program
and child-witness cap contracts. Temporary diagnostics have been removed. The
final central qualification below supersedes this failed intermediate attempt.

Terra's source review identified the omitted-`@X` child-admission gap before
central execution. The explicit child-witness correction and its five
regression contracts are included in this source freeze.

to check this cap independently of final outer execution.
