# Life F: microbiology dimensions and physical domains

F fixes reachable wrong answers in binary-fission and serial-dilution requests.
The E binary read `1.2.3 cells` as 1.2, read a negative or fractional serial-step
count from an unsigned suffix, and returned a minutes value when hours or weeks
were explicitly requested. F binds complete numeric tokens to unique roles,
converts supported input times exactly, and carries the requested generation-time
unit into the verified kernel. E remains the live packet until root promotes
this isolated, checked successor; no frozen E files were overwritten.

## What is proved

Seven newly scanned laws make the new model explicit:

- Successful equal-factor serial dilution has positive factor, denominator at
  least one, and a dilution fraction in (0,1].
- Admitted binary-fission growth has initial count at least zero, doubling time
  greater than zero and elapsed time at least zero.
- A zero initial population stays zero in the trajectory model.
- Scaling elapsed time and doubling time by the same positive factor leaves
  the trajectory unchanged.
- Generation-time value in a supported output unit multiplied by minutes per
  unit equals the minute-valued model.
- Exact generation time implies positive elapsed/initial values, increasing
  population, and a positive integer m with final/initial=2^m and
  output × minutes-per-unit × m = elapsed minutes.
- Approximate generation time implies those physical domains and absolute
  error at most |model|/1000 + 1/1000000000.

Successful old growth/generation statements retain their model equalities and
now include the new domain checks. The model assumes constant binary fission;
it does not claim real cultures obey that model indefinitely. Text recognition
and model applicability remain untrusted. Serial factors and step counts are
bounded integer roles (at most 10000 steps/output digits), not fractional
products or uncertain observations.
