# Physical families, Codex continuation

This packet owns quantity and its variants, acoustics, electromagnetic and
optical calculations, energy, geometry and statistics, mechanics, and space.
The work is still in progress. Nothing here is a final fleet score.

Each completion below describes its frozen source and native packet. Later
continuations may reopen those paths after runtime captures the earlier

Four implementation owners cover the families in those groups: quantity with

connected to dispatch and packaging.

## What the new baselines changed

The old universal zero-wrong handoff is not a usable baseline. Fresh runs of
the warm binaries found 13 wrong held-out optics answers across the first two
versions, 13 in astronomy v1, 8 in astronomy v2, and 7 in energy v2. These are
older recognizers preserving defects which later versions had already fixed.
The chosen repair completes the shared interpretation across the public
variant identities. A specialist's name does not require preserving a wrong
answer.

The extensions address information that existing recognizers silently ignored:
battery efficiency and usable capacity, statistical weights, lens units and
which distance is being requested. Each extension needs a checked calculation,
explicit refusal conditions, and acceptance by the actual runtime. New kinds
are declared in the family's `capabilities.json`; those declarations describe
the contract but are not per-answer proofs.
