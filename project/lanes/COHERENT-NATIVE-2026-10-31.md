## Admission still open

The real U8 modeling baseline completed two dev questions, then timed out on the
third at the unchanged 30-second limit. Eleven dev questions and all heldout
baseline questions remain unmeasured. One diagnostic replay of the unchanged
question, under the same limit, also timed out. Its stack captured standing-family
module imports on the query executor alongside background reader compilation.
Source inspection confirms that constructor readiness does not await those tasks
and that a fresh private workspace can rebuild shared public reader tables.
This does not establish a parser defect or a deadlock.

No timeout was relabeled as a decline, no gold was removed and no deadline was
raised. Head-relative DOMAIN/FALSEYES and complete six-criterion admission remain
incomplete. The useful next fix is explicit bounded readiness and manufactured,
source-bound shared reader artifacts, while moving the already enumerated routes
to native/extracted kernels and direct shared-store adapters. Other remaining
work is kept in the [live roadmap](../../native/docs/ROADMAP.md), including history
policy, wider reader coverage, prepared computation and upstream adoption.
