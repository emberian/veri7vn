## Performance follow-up, not a new feature wave

A memory-only restriction originally created a redundant fuel/deadline ancestor.
The corrected context now shares the same work identity and narrows only the
memory ledger. The narrow same-CPU comparison improved the large-graph median
by 11.4% but worsened p95; it does not close the earlier regression.

Ember's latency goal is the cost the CPU actually needs, not a one-second timeout
or a one-millisecond success threshold. Next measurements should separate raw
typed computation, checking, admission, dispatch and encoding; count allocations
and intermediate work; and use symmetric warmup/output lifetimes. Packed reader
representations and eliminating repeated work take priority over raising limits.
