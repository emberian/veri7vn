The model pairs equal-length finite lists by position, preserving duplicate
observations. One pass computes n, sum x, sum y, sum x² and sum xy. Population
covariance requires n>0; sample covariance requires n>1. A fitted line requires
n>0 and n sum x² − (sum x)²>0, the positive centered spread condition. Unequal
list lengths are refused before zip can discard observations.

The intended consequences connect the actual `line_fit` result to zero residual
sum and zero x-weighted residual sum, then establish the squared-error
decomposition against any alternative line and global minimality. Centered
product/square expansions connect the one-pass formulas to their statistical
definitions. `paired_line_reply_normal` and `paired_line_reply_minimal` connect
the consumer reply to the same returned coefficients. These statements pass both the isolated diagnostic and the actual staged production target described below.

The staged consumer operations are population/sample covariance, scalar regression
slope/intercept/prediction, and a full line response naming both coefficients
without a misleading single scalar value. The reader reuses the complete exact-list parser, refuses unmatched lists and unread paired modifiers, and retains list multiplicity. Weighted or filtered regression is not part of this
first finite packet. Exact reference cases and independent generated rational families are evaluated below. The checked algebra uses exact rationals; public numeric text rounds to four decimal places. A full line has two coefficients and deliberately has no single scalar `value`.
