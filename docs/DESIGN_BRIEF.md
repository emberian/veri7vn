# Design brief

Build a useful continuation of Oracle7: an Isabelle-native environment whose author
can create, diagnose, compose, improve and deploy specialists without its original
implementers. Cover readers, mathematics, sources, synthesis, routing, dialogue,
state and growth; a small verified fragment is not a replacement product.

The accepted [architecture](ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL.md) combines open native HOL
meanings, generated executable authority and typed computational families. Serious
budgeted proof search runs in the end-user installation. Search may be heuristic;
its accepted results need adequate checked evidence. Interpretation has a declared
formal relation without claiming to prove human intention. Owned algorithms require
real implementation bridges; wrapping Python is a compatibility adapter.

## Required engineering outcomes

- One canonical package connects source, meaning, implementation, language,
  examples, assumptions, evidence, performance and dependencies.
- Native definitions/theorems remain extensible. Ordinary recipes and plans reuse
  existing kernels; new executable carriers/code setup stage qualified bundles.
- Source reports, adopted premises, empirical observations and mathematical claims
  retain their exact scope. Witnesses do not prove completeness or optimality.
- Generated authority controls actual publication/state/effect paths. Host and
  native-object bridges have explicit trust contracts and consumers.
- Authoring exposes useful candidates before universal proofs, while keeping
  admission, operational qualification and activation separate.
- Deployment supplies pinned runtime/heaps and editable sources without hidden
  theory builds. Measure startup, admission, search, data access, execution,
  checking, memory and I/O on representative workloads.
- Migration records preserved behavior, deliberate repairs, unavailable data and
  unresolved obligations. The six siblings remain scoped investigations.

[PLAN](../project/PLAN.md) and [acceptance criteria](product/ACCEPTANCE.md) define
completion; [DECISIONS](../project/DECISIONS.md) and [tasks](../project/tasks.json)
own design/status. Research probes establish only their stated results. Native
implementation lives under `src/isabelle/`; the [prototype recovery note](design/prototype-cleanup.md)
explains why the earlier expression/DAG/C experiment is not its foundation.
