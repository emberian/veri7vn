# Exact quantities and conditional physical models

The native family lifts a finite unit/spring subset of audited source behavior. Broader domain profiles remain required; no manifest-wide completion follows from these kernels.

## Meaning and algorithms

`Domain_Quantities` separates dimension-tagged linear quantities, absolute temperature points and temperature differences. Exact rational SI coordinates support length, mass, US volume, area, speed, fixed duration, pressure, energy, power, data, Newton and Newton/metre. Conventions include US gallon, thermochemical calorie, conventional mmHg/BTU; a day is86400s, not a calendar operation. External constant provenance remains a source obligation.

`linear_conversion_refinement` identifies conversion with same-dimension coordinate equality; `linear_conversion_composes` gives direct/composed equality. Temperature point refinement/composition require nonnegative Kelvin; differences use a separate linear operation. Affine points cannot masquerade as deltas.

`spring_kernel_refinement` covers force/extension/stiffness/energy by Hooke's law or2E=kx², positive stiffness and nonnegative magnitude. Zero denominators reject; upstream extension/stiffness can divide by zero. Period/frequency requiring sqrt/pi approximation are outside this exact leaf. Mathematical consequences remain conditional on an adopted ideal model, not material measurements.

`Domain_Plans` instantiates `typed_plan_bundle`; `domain_dispatch_type`, `domain_dispatch_input_types` and `quantity_node_semantics` connect actual operation signatures and inherited conditions. `run_quantity_plan` adds the ideal-model premise to every spring node; `spring_model_premise_retained` preserves it. `Quantity_Specialists.spring_energy_package` composes10cm→metres and200N/m→1J, retaining model105/input700. Wrong unit edges reject before primitives; missing adoption blocks.

## Managed execution

`Domain_Bundle` supplies opaque instance/request/output, fixed executable pin, admitted `domain_execution_refinement`, bounded typed projection, positive modeled cost, invocation identity and release. `DomainCall` holds operation/typed values/conditions/optional model ID; `DomainAnswer`, `DomainRejected` and `DomainBlocked` remain distinct. Closed failures are `MalformedCarrier`, `QuantityMismatch`, `BelowAbsoluteZero`, `InvalidSpringParameters`; timeout/materialization/released handles are operational failures. Charge is a syntax-based polynomial, not a time/memory theorem.

The request freezes `domain_call_meaning`; an adopted model uses `domain_model_call_meaning force energy call` and a separately admitted universally quantified conditional contract under `ideal_spring_model force energy`. Model/condition IDs must reference actual complete propositions and source owners. `Veri7vn_Domain_Conditions` parses the original source slice in its actual managed receiver and checks proposition identity, role and dependencies. Equal bytes in another owner or same text in a different receiver cannot substitute.

`Veri7vn_Domain_Request` retains exact input/template, both contracts, canonical identity and ordered conditions. Independent consumer leases survive producer release; invalidation denies immediately, storage drains after final lease. Snapshots compare full proposition/owner/context correspondence, not numeric IDs. `prepare_into` records newly published objects in caller cleanup roots before interruptibility returns; lower bundle roots protect layered publication.

## Rational and display boundary

The checked rational adapter converts explicit reflected `Rat.Frct` pairs to ordinary total `Rat.Fract` using kernel equalities and fixed numeral conversions; it retains original=converted endpoints. It rejects computed/nested abstract pairs before expansion, bounding original/converted terms and time. Noncanonical2/4, negative1/−2 and zero-denominator1/0 use actual HOL meanings. Installed RC1 Codegen/Computations excludes abstract input constructors; rational ordering relies on canonical pairs. Local math/domain converter factoring remains a possible refinement, requiring consumer checks.

`Domain_Checked_Display` decodes a bounded numerical sentence with units, point/delta distinction, exact rational, premise count and model qualifier. Count alone does not identify premises. `Veri7vn_Domain_Display` compares decoded result with the same actual request/output and ordered conditions, projecting full premise cterms, original slices/owners/spans beside escaped HTML. `Veri7vn_Domain_Native_Gate` retains accepted implementation/model/render contracts, certified target and independent condition leases. A certified target is syntax associated with runtime refinement, not a per-answer kernel theorem. Neither leaf publishes or allocates service events.
