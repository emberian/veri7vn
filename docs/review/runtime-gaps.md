## R1 — P0: routing authority needs typed, scoped dispositions

Check recoverable miss, invalid domain, valid alternative, disagreement and acknowledgement beside blocked assertion. C03; PO-01/04/08; ACC-03/04/05/08.

## R2 — P0: composite turns need an outcome tree and dependency order

w529:92–114 answers sequential parts in one session; w533/w537 sometimes answer the whole then retry parts in other sessions. Any-answer status and flattened prose lose partial scope and can duplicate effects.

Elaborate a request graph retaining per-node readings, outcomes, evidence and dependencies. Dependent parts consume accepted structured bindings; independent nodes may run concurrently. Specify partial commit behavior and never replay whole-turn effects while splitting. Check “capital of Austria and its currency,” independent mixed-success parts and cancelled dependent calculations. C03/C05; PO-02/03/04/10; ACC-04/05/11.

## R3 — P0: only final, licensed results should update answer-derived context

w529/w534 update context before outer w540 may withhold; `T/chat_context_v2.py:152–190` extracts entities/numbers from answer prose. Stage typed answer deltas and adopt only after final arbitration/admission. Record utterance/topic history separately. Context consumes stable entities/quantities, not relinked first sentences. A retracted answer cannot seed “square that” or “where was she born?”; rejected candidates remain diagnostic. C04/C05; PO-04/09/10; ACC-04/07/11.
