# What Oracle7 is, as shipped

A map of the system under `upstream/`, written from the code rather than from
its README, with the file that holds each part. Paths are relative to
`upstream/root/` unless they start with `upstream/`. The audited revision is
`c5d6a29d5bc5e2525078e5a53afa1abf659e4d69`.

## One sentence

Oracle7 is a priority-ordered cascade of guarded handlers in front of three
record stores, with a specialist mechanism that may only fill the cascade's
declines, an arbitration rule for when specialists disagree, and an exam that
admits new specialists only when they add right answers without adding wrong
ones.

## The cascade (the "head")

`fable-content/oracle-m-series-2026-08-15/self_loop/admitted/oracle_release_runtime_w540.py`
is the tip of a chain of **346 Python classes**, each subclassing the previous
one and overriding `chat(text, session_id)`. A class ("fold") does one of:

- **narrow**: call the parent, then withhold its answer when a guard fires
  (w540 withholds three kinds of false yes found by a sealed test);
- **route before**: try its own handler and fall back to the parent;
- **rewrite and redispatch**: rewrite the question into a form the chain can
  read and dispatch again (`oracle_release_runtime_ao.py`, generated by the
  "meta-fold" from 161 unanswered conversational turns);
- **state only**: record something and pass through.

Guards are regular expressions over the question and inspections of the
parent's reply (`path`, `status`, `response`). The chain needs
`sys.setrecursionlimit(20000)` and runs under `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`, which means
some answer somewhere depends on hash iteration order. The whole chain is about
29,700 lines. `HEAD_MANIFEST.json` lists the admitted order; `audit/folds/`
in this repository catalogues every fold.

The base of the chain, `wander-train/oracle_release_runtime.py`, routes to
three engines in order: a session-relational IR pipeline for things the user
introduced (`stage5m13e2_construction_kernel_k7b.py`, `stage5m13e4_migration2.py`,
`stage5m13e6_realizer2b.py`), the ConceptNet adapter
(`oracle_release_conceptnet.py`), and the anchored document runtime over
DBpedia/Wikipedia/OpenStax sentences with tiered cue lists
(`stage5m13d2_anchored_general_document.py` and the `aa` fold's mined cues).

## The stores

| Store | Where | Size | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wikidata relations | `relstore/relations_v1.sqlite3`: `entity(qid,label,enwiki)`, `fact(s,p,o,lit)` | 3.6 GB | the relation readers |
| Wikipedia, DBpedia abstracts, OpenStax, Simple English Wikipedia | `fable-content/oracle-m-series-2026-08-15/m13e*` builds | 29 GB | document routes, bench routes |
| WordNet, ConceptNet, Wiktionary | same tree | | definitions, commonsense |

The shipped runtime refers to `relations_v2.sqlite3` and `names_v1.sqlite3`;
neither is in the drop. Version 1 of the relation store is present.

## The relation readers (the biggest answering route)

On the 924-question core panel, 554 questions are answered; **204 of those by
`organ:relation`**, 11 by `exact_router`. Two readers:

- `wander-train/exact_router_v1.py`: 32 admitted `(asked noun, content word) →
  property` patterns over 13 properties, mined from 124,000 TriviaQA questions
  and admitted at ≥ 90% precision on both halves. The entity linker
  (`entity_linker_v2.py`) proposes at most three candidates; the question is
  answered only when every candidate carrying the property agrees on one value.
  Refuses yes/no questions, hidden hops ("the brother-in-law of X"), blank
  nodes, and patterns that disagree about the property.
- `wander-train/general_reader_v1.py`: phrasing-independent. Finds the named
  entities (exact Wikipedia titles), reads every fact one step away, and
  answers only when the relation is cued by the question's words, the answer
  is the type asked for, and exactly one candidate survives.

Both carry a receipt (subject, property, value, ids) in the reply.

## Specialists

`specialists/<id>/` holds 305 manifests (267 admitted in the release, 23 failed
exams published with reasons). Each is:

- `manifest.json`: `accept.kinds` (the moves it may answer), `declines_in_domain`
  (the moves it may decline, only when `context_regex` matches), `routing_cues`,
  budgets (p50 latency, store size), sources and licences, and the panel/scorer.
- an organ module: `class X: def answer(self, text, session_id) -> {"kind",
  "status", "response", "value"} | None`. Regex recognition, then a table lookup
  or a formula. `narrow_v1.py` (shared) makes organs stay out of word problems.
- a panel (dev and held-out items with `q`, `valid`, `expect`/`a`) and a scorer
  `ok(item, reply) -> (good, asserted)`.
- `verdict_dev.json`, `verdict_heldout.json`, and a card in `_cards/`.

Composition (`upstream/workbench/exam.py`, class `Loaded`, and the release's
spec runtime): the head answers first; a specialist is consulted **only when the
head withheld for lack of a reading** (`fillable(path)`), never when it
answered and never when a check stopped it. On the core panel 142 answers come
from `specialist_call` and 84 from the `network` (below).

The 305 manifests collapse into about 81 semantic shapes (table lookup with a
unit, formula with declared assumptions, rule table, ordered facts, graph
algorithm, finite probability, site/sensor state). `docs/coverage/` holds that
classification.

## Arbitration and fusion

- `specialists/_gate/combo_runtime_v1.py`: up to four specialists run together.
  Every organ is asked. Conflicts resolve in a fixed order: uses more of the
  question's own numbers; does not invent givens; an in-domain decline with a
  checked reason beats a foreign answer; the organ whose context regex matches
  the asking sentence; the user's pick order. If the two best still tie and
  state different numbers, the reply says they disagree and shows both.
- `specialists/_gate/network_v3.py`: specialist **fusion**. A pool of typed
  quantities (the givens plus every verified intermediate); any specialist whose
  typed slots can be filled is asked in its own proven phrasing and its reply
  parsed by unit; core math finishes. Breadth-first to depth 3; at the
  shallowest depth where any plan completes, every complete plan must agree
  within 0.5% or the system declines. Path memory (`path_hrr_v1.py`, holographic
  reduced representations, D=1024) only orders the search; it never decides.

## Mathematics, code and word problems

- `wander-train/math_organ_v3.py` (516 lines): directive + expression
  (solve/simplify/factor/expand/evaluate/differentiate/integrate/limit), every
  result independently checked, method cited. Uses SymPy underneath.
- `wander-train/math_solver_v3.py` (992 lines): competition-style problems,
  answered only when one typed family reads all of it and the answer passes that
  family's own check (50-digit evaluation, back-substitution, brute force).
- `wander-train/word_problems_v1.py`: the head's word-problem organ, which
  `narrow_v1` protects from the specialists.
- `wander-train/code_organ_v3.py` (488 lines): programs as typed moves over a
  verified function library, checked by execution; verified programs join the
  library.
- Precision guards (`precision_guard:P…`, `G…`, `Q…` in paths) withhold
  arithmetic read from English when a sign, a unit or an English word was
  mistaken for algebra; 114 of the 924 core-panel items end there.

## Growth

- The **exam** (`upstream/workbench/exam.py`): six criteria on a held-out set
  used once and bound to the folder's hash. Domain gain ≥ max(3, 2% of n); no new
  false yes; wrong ≤ max(1, 2% of n); takes at most max(5, 1%) of the 924 core
  questions; leaves identity questions alone; median latency within budget.
- The **meta-fold** (M53): clusters unanswered turns and generates rewrite folds
  (`oracle_release_runtime_ao.py`), admitted by the same held-out rule.
- **Path memory** was built and measured but is switched off; its one solid
  finding shipped as a fixed rule (relation specialists beat other routes on
  questions they recognize).
- The **Workbench** (`upstream/workbench/`): local page to write a specialist,
  run dev exams freely and the held-out exam once, connect sensors, MCP servers,
  SSH hosts and files as readers, keep conversations, and anchor a specialist's
  fingerprint on Solana.

## Where veri7vn starts

The route shares above say what to lift first: relation reading (204 + 11 of
554 answers), specialist composition and arbitration (142 + 84), the cascade
itself (every answer passes through it), and then fusion, mathematics and the
document route. The exam is the growth mechanism the author already knows, so
veri7vn keeps it as the definition of done.
